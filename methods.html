0. 이 문서 사용법 · 검증 등급

왜 이 문서가 「잉카」 규격서에 붙는가

「잉카」 규격서는 완성된 영상 1편을 프레임 단위로 되돌린 것입니다. 즉 "이 채널이 실제로 무엇을 했는가"만 담고 있고, 다른 사람들은 같은 문제를 어떻게 풀었는가는 비어 있었습니다. 이 문서가 그 빈칸입니다.

10인을 고른 기준은 유명세가 아니라 검증 가능한 숫자를 공개했는가입니다. "몇 컷 생성해서 몇 컷 썼는가", "얼마 들었는가", "몇 시간 걸렸는가" 를 본인이 밝힌 사람 위주로 골랐습니다. 숫자 없이 소감만 있는 사람은 아무리 유명해도 넣지 않았습니다.

먼저 읽어야 할 한계 — 원문 페이지를 직접 열지 못했습니다

이 문서를 만든 세션은 외부 웹페이지 직접 접속(egress)이 정책상 차단돼 있었습니다(프록시 403). 그래서 각 기사·게시물을 열어 통독한 것이 아니라, 검색 결과에 노출된 원문 카드와 완전일치 구문 재검색으로 교차 확인했습니다. A등급은 문장 자체가 카드에 그대로 찍힌 것이고, B등급은 매체가 직접 인용으로 보도한 것입니다. 인용문을 그대로 재인용하시려면 옆의 출처 링크를 직접 열어 대조하십시오. 자세한 것은 15절에 적었습니다.

실무 환산 — 「잉카」 편에 적용하면

「잉카」 1편의 추정 실제 장면 수는 약 115개입니다(규격서 17절). 채택률 중앙값 7.9%를 적용하면:

필요 생성 수 = 115컷 ÷ 0.079 ≒ 1,456회
보수적 가정(채택률 4%)  = 115 ÷ 0.04 ≒ 2,875회
낙관적 가정(채택률 14%) = 115 ÷ 0.14 ≒   821회

→ 19분 30초 롱폼 1편의 생성 예산은 약 800 ~ 2,900회 사이로 잡는다.
→ 이 범위를 모르고 "컷당 2~3번이면 되겠지"로 잡으면 일정이 5~10배 어긋난다.

주의. 위 계산은 공개된 3개 사례의 채택률을 「잉카」의 장면 수에 대입한 추정입니다. 「잉카」 편의 실제 생성 횟수는 측정되지 않았습니다 — 완성본만 계측했기 때문입니다.

방법 — 4단계

  1. 러프 스크립트를 사람이 쓴다. (Kalshi 는 다룰 시장 목록만 줬고 각본은 본인이 짰다)
  2. Gemini 로 프롬프트 대량 변환 — 컷마다 1프롬프트, 한 번에 5개씩.
  3. Flow 에서 fast 모드로 5개 동시 생성. 안 맞으면 그 프롬프트를 Gemini 에 다시 붙여 수정 요청 → 재생성. 기다리는 동안 다음 컷으로 건너뛰어(leapfrog) 놀지 않는다.
  4. Premiere / CapCut 에서 편집. 생성 단계에서 연결을 맞추려 하지 않는다.

핵심은 3번의 leapfrog 입니다. 생성 대기 시간이 이 워크플로의 진짜 병목이고, 그 시간을 다음 컷 프롬프트로 채우는 것이 2~3일 완성을 만든 실제 이유입니다.

생성 예산 · 페인트아웃 사전 점검 (기획 단계에서 LLM 에 붙여넣기)

Act as a generative-video line producer.

MY TARGET: <N> seconds of finished film, <M> cuts.

1. BUDGET THE RAW
   - Compute the generation budget at THREE keep rates: 14%, 8%, 4%.
   - Report it as "generations needed" and "hours of raw footage".
   - State plainly which number I should put in the schedule (use 8%).

2. FLAG THE PAINT-OUT
   For my concept below, list EVERY shot where the model is likely to
   hallucinate one of: a human face, a hand with fingers, readable text,
   a logo, a repeated background extra, a reflection.
   Mark each one "PAINT-OUT REQUIRED" and estimate roto minutes.

3. DECIDE, PER FLAGGED SHOT

가면·의상은 한 번 만들면 영원히 같은 얼굴입니다. 시드도, 프롬프트 문구도 흔들리지 않습니다. 그리고 부수 효과가 하나 더 있습니다 — 그 캐릭터의 IP를 본인이 실제로 소유하게 됩니다.

그대로 쓰는 프롬프트 09

실물 선제작 → 레퍼런스 고정 파이프라인

STEP 1 — BUILD THE PHYSICAL THING FIRST
  A mask, a helmet, a costume piece, a prop. Even a rough one.
  (If you truly cannot build it: buy one, or 3D-print it, or sculpt it.
   A physical object photographs consistently. A description does not.)

STEP 2 — SHOOT THE REFERENCE PLATE
  Neutral grey background. Soft even light, no colored gels.
  Five angles, same distance, same lens: front / 3-4 left / profile left /
  3-4 right / top. Plus one detail macro of the texture.
  Name them: ref/<char>_front.png, ref/<char>_34l.png, ...

STEP 3 — LOCK THE CHARACTER LINE
  Write ONE sentence describing the object, and paste it into EVERY prompt,
  word for word, forever:
  "<CHAR>: <silhouette>, <one material>, <one color>, <one damage mark>"
  The damage mark (a chip, a scratch, a stain) is what the model latches
  onto for identity. Never omit it.

STEP 4 — GENERATE with ref images attached + the character line.
  Never generate a character from text alone after this point.

STEP 5 — TREAT THE OUTPUT AS DAILIES, NOT AS THE FILM
  Storyboard, blocking, continuity, editing rhythm, grade:
  all still done by hand. Premiere / Resolve / After Effects / Blender.

「잉카」 편과의 관계 — 규격서 §10 은 인물을 줄이는 전략이었습니다(정면 얼굴 5회, 얼굴 예산 4.4%). Cardoza 의 방법은 인물을 늘려야 하는 기획에 필요한 반대편 도구입니다. 두 편(잉카·아즈텍) 비교 이후 캐릭터가 필요한 주제로 확장할 때 이 절차를 씁니다.

14. 통합 마스터 프롬프트 — 규격서 + 10인 방법 합본

「잉카」 규격서의 측정값과 위 10인의 방법을 하나의 실행 가능한 지시문으로 합쳤습니다. LLM 에 통째로 붙여넣고 < > 안만 바꿔 쓰십시오.

MASTER · 전체 합본 
You are the production system for a long-form AI-generated documentary video.
Follow this spec exactly. It is derived from (a) a frame-by-frame teardown of a
finished 19m29s episode and (b) the published methods of ten working AI
video creators. Where the two conflict, the measured teardown wins.

=====================================================================
PHASE 0 — DECIDE BEFORE GENERATING ANYTHING          [08 Mooser/Lyonne, 10 Nelson]
=====================================================================
0.1 Final distribution: < personal channel / brand / broadcast / theatrical >
    -> if anything above "personal channel", the model must have a
       documented, licensed training corpus. Write the vendor's answer down.
0.2 Own the IP first: characters, script, world, rough cut are MINE.
    Every asset exports to a model-independent format (png/mp4/json/txt).
0.3 Name a second vendor for every step. A step with no second vendor is
    a single point of failure. Write it in the risk row.

=====================================================================
PHASE 1 — TIME STRUCTURE                             [teardown 4, 6 + 04 Neubert]
=====================================================================
1.1 LOCK THE MUSIC / narration bed FIRST. Nothing visual before this.
1.2 Chapters: < 12 > chapters. Target chapter length 82.7 s
    (measured MEAN of the reference episode, ending chapter excluded;
     97.4 s if you count all 12). Do not free-run the length.
    Scene length below is a MEDIAN - do not mix the two statistics.
1.3 Scene length: median 11.9 s. Longest tolerable 25 s.
1.4 Narration occupies ~64% of runtime at ~189 words/min.
    Plan 14 deliberate silences. Silence is a designed element, not a gap.
1.5 Write the beat timecodes NOW. Shots get filled in later.

=====================================================================
PHASE 2 — SHOT LIST AND BUDGET                       [02 shy kids, 01 Accetturo]
=====================================================================
2.1 Break chapters into shots. Expect ~115 shots for a 19-20 min episode.
2.2 GENERATION BUDGET at a keep rate of 8% (median of three public cases):
    generations_needed = shots / 0.08          # ~1,450 for 115 shots
    Plan the range 800 - 2,900 and say which number is in the schedule.
2.3 For every shot, flag likely failure: face / fingers / text / logo /
    repeated extra / reflection. For each flagged shot choose ONE:
      (a) paint it out in post  — budget the roto minutes, or
      (b) REDESIGN so it never enters frame  <- prefer this, it is cheaper
2.4 Roto / paint / grade is a REQUIRED schedule line, not a contingency.

=====================================================================
PHASE 3 — LIVE ACTION vs GENERATION                             [06 McNitt]
=====================================================================
For each shot ask: "could a real camera be here, with a real performer?"
  YES -> shoot it. NO -> generate it.
Generate only what no lens can reach: inside a body, inside a star,
deep time, impossible scale moves.
Grade the generated plates TO the live-action, never the reverse.

=====================================================================
PHASE 4 — VISUAL SPEC (copy verbatim, change only the nouns)     [teardown 1-3]
=====================================================================
STYLE BLOCK — append this identical text to EVERY image/video prompt:
  painterly landscape, strong atmospheric perspective, layered haze,
  distant scale, no readable text, no on-screen graphics,
  < LIGHT DIRECTIVE FOR THIS CHAPTER >, cinematic film still,
  < NN >mm lens, subtle grain

LIGHT DIRECTIVE BY NARRATIVE PHASE (warm-pixel ratio is the story engine):
  rise / golden age  -> "warm golden hour"
       measured warm-pixel ratio 36.6-56.9% (median 51.7)
  collapse           -> "cold overcast, blue hour"
       measured warm-pixel ratio 7.8-19.7% (the floor of the episode)
  legacy / return    -> "warm golden hour"
       measured warm-pixel ratio 56.9% (recovery)
Measure the warm ratio on the finished cut. If the curve is flat, the
episode has no emotion no matter how good the individual shots are.

FACE BUDGET — hard cap:
  named-character frontal face shots: <= 5 in the whole episode (~2% runtime)
  all visible faces including crowds: <= 4.4% of runtime
  everything else: crop the head, object insert, back shot, silhouette,
  motion blur, tiny-in-frame, stylized portrait.

=====================================================================
PHASE 5 — PROMPT GENERATION                                  [01 Accetturo]
=====================================================================
Convert every shot into exactly one prompt. Return 5 at a time, then stop.
Each prompt describes the scene as if the model has NO context of the shot
before or after it: restate location, light, wardrobe, camera, lens, mood
EVERY time. Never write "same as previous" or "the man from before".
Order: [SHOT SIZE][SUBJECT full description][ONE ACTION][LOCATION]
       [TIME/LIGHT][CAMERA MOVE][LENS][AUDIO][no subtitles] + STYLE BLOCK
While a batch renders, write the next batch. Never idle.

=====================================================================
PHASE 6 — MOTION AND TRANSITIONS                  [teardown 7,9,12 + 03 Trillo]
=====================================================================
6.1 Smallest possible motion. Slow drift, slow push. Long moves break first.
    ~7% of scenes should be nearly static — that is a rest beat, keep it.
6.2 Transition mix, measured on 71 eye-verified boundaries:
      A hard cut            52.1%  (37)
      B white-out pass      8.5%   (6)   |
      C black-out pass     11.3%   (8)   |-> PASS-THROUGH = 25.4% (18)
      D occluder wipe       5.6%   (4)   |
      E cross dissolve     18.3%  (13)
      F angle change        4.2%   (3)
    PASS-THROUGH: the camera enters an occluder (cloud, arch, tunnel, a
    horse's body, smoke, a passing wall) until the frame is 100% covered,
    and the cut happens inside that cover. This is how two clips from two
    unrelated seeds get joined: by deleting the information to be joined.
    Change only the occluder and it ports to any subject.
6.3 Where continuity is impossible, do not cut at all — use one continuous
    forward dolly and let space A morph into space B. Dream logic allowed.

=====================================================================
PHASE 7 — QC, TWO PASSES IN THIS ORDER              [teardown 14 + 03 Trillo]
=====================================================================
PASS 1 (reject): anatomy errors, readable garbage text, logo lookalikes,
  broken continuity in a shot the viewer will study, flicker, warped hands.
PASS 2 (rescue): of what pass 1 rejected, ask ONE question —
  "does this strangeness serve the subject?" If yes, keep it.
  Delete-by-default loses the only thing the medium does better than a camera.

=====================================================================
PHASE 8 — LOG EVERY SHOT                                       [07 Clark]
=====================================================================
shot_id, chapter, prompt (full), negative, model, version, seed,
ref_images, ref_license, generations, selected, post[], approved_by, approved_at
A shot with no log is not a deliverable.
If model+version+seed is incomplete, mark the shot NOT REPRODUCIBLE.

=====================================================================
PHASE 9 — BEFORE PUBLISHING                              [this repo, 2026-09]
=====================================================================
9.1 Have someone (or some agent) OTHER than the author audit the numbers.
    Self-review of a self-authored report left 47 errors last time.
9.2 If the underlying data was regenerated, re-extract EVERY timecode and
    EVERY example quoted in the body. Old data leaves old citations behind.
9.3 Check commit author email is a noreply address, not a personal one.
9.4 Check no third party's handle / name is inside subtitles or captions.

이 10인은 객관적 순위가 아닙니다. 선정 기준은 딱 하나 — 검증 가능한 숫자를 공개했는가 였습니다. 숫자를 공개하지 않은 뛰어난 제작자가 훨씬 많고, 영어권 매체 검색으로 골랐기 때문에 한국·중국·일본어권 제작자가 구조적으로 빠져 있습니다. 이 편향을 감추지 않고 적어 둡니다.
